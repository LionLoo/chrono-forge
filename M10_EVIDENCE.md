## Locked M10 catalogue contract

- `tests/fixtures/m10-scenarios.ts` owns 25 unique four-word seeds. Every world runs through at least 60 accelerated civil years using the production simulation and all integrated authorities.
- Checkpoints at years 5, 25, and 60 validate invariants and continuing deterministic work. The final state locks a canonical hash and per-system operation totals only after semantic assertions pass.
- The catalogue collectively requires multi-generation identity, cultural breadth, relationship/family activity, factual history, contextual technology divergence, modeled crisis/recovery, multiple societies, construction/economy activity, at least one active factual advanced city, and M9-capable worlds. Dedicated earlier long tests retain rare controlled paths and the full 240-year space minimum without duplicating their cost.
- A deterministic subset must serialize, semantically reload, and reproduce the same canonical hash and bytes.
- No camera state, elapsed-time budget, unrecorded fixture reward, or manual civilization outcome may influence a catalogue result.

## Controlled collapse and autonomous recovery

- `tests/fixtures/m10-collapse-recovery.ts` locks cooperative-profile seed `[1,2,3,4]`, a severity-100 supply shortage at tick 150,000, fixed 1,000-tick observation windows, and exact pre-shock, strongest-decline, recovery, and 600,000-tick final hashes plus per-system operation totals.
- The same production world declines from 17 living residents to zero, from 923 bounded-window production to zero, from 2,732 total shelter condition to a 2,105 trough, and from 100 stability to zero. Normal bounded systems then record recovery at tick 160,000 and reach 35 residents, 5,428 shelter condition, 2,257 peak post-recovery window production, and 90 stability at the final horizon.
- The test requires one factual `supply-shortage`, `settlement-collapsed`, and `settlement-recovered` chronology with death before migration before recovery. It rejects every M5 crisis during the recovery path, every M6 conflict/conquest/refugee/territory shortcut, pending intervention, fixture reset, and invariant violation.
- The strongest integrated low point canonically serializes and reloads byte-for-byte, and the reloaded world reproduces the exact authoritative recovery state. Production evidence is always measured across complete fixed windows; shelter decline uses authoritative condition because this seed's damaged shelter remains operational rather than losing binary capacity.

## Relationship lifecycle and factual biography

- `tests/fixtures/m10-relationship-lifecycle.ts` locks a 39-year controlled society and a separate production-world recorded-shortage path. The controlled path creates real rivalry and family estrangement, holds both adverse states for at least ten civil years, reconciles through ordinary healthy pulses, locks exact hashes and operation counts, and rejects duplicate estrangement/rivalry/enmity events.
- The production world uses seed `[7,11,13,17]` on a 32x32 map, warms for 20 civil years, then records severity-70 supply shortages every two years for 20 years. At tick 200,000 four factual family estrangements are active with exact event IDs and biography references. A canonical snapshot reloads byte-for-byte and reproduces the uninterrupted world through all four exact reconciliations by tick 235,000.
- The stable 60-year river-kinship world locks a 52-year close friendship, a long-term partnership and matching household history, death-backed widowhood, a culturally eligible communal-guardianship child arrival, and recognizable generations 0 through 3. Every retained biography/shared-event ID resolves to the matching immutable world event and participant; active/archive/shared-event/household/biography storage remains within declared caps.
- `tests/long/m45-relationships.long.test.ts` remains the qualifying three-seed/60-year breadth catalogue for multi-generation lineage, 20-year bonds, household change, lifecycle event breadth, graph caps, canonical hashes, operation totals, arbitrary tick partitions, and mid-history reload. Rare controlled evidence is reused rather than duplicating its long-run cost.
- Production now permits the explicit reconciliation threshold first and otherwise preserves an estranged family edge, preventing the former annual estranged-to-rival/enemy oscillation. At that relationship checkpoint, the behavioral change affected only concord-orbit after its tick-600,000 relationship events: six invalid society transition operations disappeared while all physical/alien semantics and the second M9 seed remained unchanged. The later v13 prose audit intentionally renews canonical world hashes wherever saved event wording changes while leaving the already-reviewed operation totals and semantic outcomes intact.

## Factual event descriptions and recap provenance

- `src/simulation/events/index.ts` owns one closed catalogue for every player-visible world, settlement, society, relationship, development, civilization, and space event kind. Event admission rejects unknown kinds, untrimmed or overlong prose, missing sentence punctuation, placeholder values, legacy `Body-N` labels, known generic templates, and summaries that fail to name typed non-world subjects or targets.
- Every registered kind is driven through the production `appendWorldEvent` path in focused coverage. The 25-seed catalogue independently rejects generic normalized collisions, invalid kinds or descriptions, and non-canonical reloads across generated long worlds. Timeline, biography, relationship, recap, civilization, development, and space projections continue to quote the same immutable event copy.
- Snapshot v13 migrates v12 and older saves by rewriting only deficient event summaries and matching bounded agent-memory or focus-recap copies. Migration preserves IDs, kinds, ticks, typed references, amounts, structured child eligibility, RNG streams, and every other simulation field; it adds, removes, and replays no event. A legacy focus recap changes only after its raw ID/tick/summary exactly matches the checksummed legacy event and then must match migrated authority.
- Stored focus-history prose is displayed only when it resolves exactly to the already-loaded active-slot world. Detached, inconsistent, or unresolvable rows retain session duration, issued-tick accounting, and event IDs but expose no unverified narrative. One active snapshot is indexed once per refresh, and each history row checks at most twelve recap IDs.

## Content and asset gate

- Observable generated worlds must draw from release-quality person-name, culture-name, belief, tradition, architecture, and celestial-name vocabularies with deterministic selection and stable persisted results.
- Player-visible celestial bodies may not ship as `Body-N` placeholders.
- Snapshot v11 migrates exact M9 v10 saves by replacing only `Body-N` celestial placeholders with seed-derived names. Snapshot v12 adds authoritative settlement stage, change tick, and provenance; v11 and older migration derives the highest fact-supported stage at the saved tick without adding an event. Future observed changes remain adjacent, hysteretic, and source-backed.
- Every runtime or packaging image, icon, font, texture, sound, or music file must be covered exactly once by `assets/attribution.json`, including its source, license, creation method, and distribution permission. Dependency licenses remain covered by lockfile notices and the SBOM.
- Adding a new runtime asset without an attribution record is a test failure.
