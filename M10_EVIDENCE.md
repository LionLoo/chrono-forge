Status vocabulary:

- `baseline verified`: the system is implemented and already has focused evidence from an earlier merged milestone.
- `M10 gate`: M10 adds or reruns integrated evidence on `release/v1-content-hardening`.
- `later gate`: a named later milestone owns additional acceptance that M10 must not weaken.

## Locked M10 catalogue contract

- `tests/fixtures/m10-scenarios.ts` owns 25 unique four-word seeds. Every world runs through at least 60 accelerated civil years using the production simulation and all integrated authorities.
- Checkpoints at years 5, 25, and 60 validate invariants and continuing deterministic work. The final state locks a canonical hash and per-system operation totals only after semantic assertions pass.
- The catalogue collectively requires multi-generation identity, cultural breadth, relationship/family activity, factual history, contextual technology divergence, modeled crisis/recovery, multiple societies, construction/economy activity, at least one active factual advanced city, and M9-capable worlds. Dedicated earlier long tests retain rare controlled paths and the full 240-year space minimum without duplicating their cost.
- A deterministic subset must serialize, semantically reload, and reproduce the same canonical hash and bytes.
- No camera state, elapsed-time budget, unrecorded fixture reward, or manual civilization outcome may influence a catalogue result.

## Content and asset gate

- Observable generated worlds must draw from release-quality person-name, culture-name, belief, tradition, architecture, and celestial-name vocabularies with deterministic selection and stable persisted results.
- Player-visible celestial bodies may not ship as `Body-N` placeholders.
- Snapshot v11 migrates exact M9 v10 saves by replacing only `Body-N` celestial placeholders with seed-derived names. Snapshot v12 adds authoritative settlement stage, change tick, and provenance; v11 and older migration derives the highest fact-supported stage at the saved tick without adding an event. Future observed changes remain adjacent, hysteretic, and source-backed.
- Every runtime or packaging image, icon, font, texture, sound, or music file must be covered exactly once by `assets/attribution.json`, including its source, license, creation method, and distribution permission. Dependency licenses remain covered by lockfile notices and the SBOM.
- Adding a new runtime asset without an attribution record is a test failure.

Event-description expansion and a normalized template-breadth assertion are still required before M10 acceptance; this checkpoint makes no completion claim for that part of the row.
