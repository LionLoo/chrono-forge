Every visible person has a deterministic, bounded, stable presentation identity derived from existing factual projections or stable IDs, a readable silhouette at useful scales, non-color selection/focus cues, and an activity treatment that never claims more than authoritative task data provides.
3. Homes, civic structures, districts, roads, fields, industry, settlements, ruins, hazards, orbiting craft, colonies, and space objects use intentional era- and role-readable forms rather than generic blocks, crosses, or debug symbols. Visual state for construction, growth, danger, and damage derives only from current Worker facts.
4. Terrain, water, resources, weather, lighting, atmosphere, movement, and era transitions create a rich living world at every useful camera scale. Presentation-only variation is deterministic and camera-independent; changing camera, text scale, effects settings, or animation state never changes authoritative state or factual history.
5. Exploration is fluid and contained. A player can move from a civilization-wide overview through distinct settlements and districts to homes, buildings, and visible people, retain orientation, revisit inspected locations, and zoom at the intended pointer anchor without scrolling the document or losing primary controls.
6. Close views reveal materially richer factual presentation than wide views; wide views communicate settlement layout, civilization shape, danger, infrastructure, and space activity without becoming an indistinguishable symbol field. Detail selection is bounded and driven only by view scale and factual projections.
7. Founding, mature settlement, crisis, advanced-city, and space scenarios each have deterministic wide and close visual regression evidence. Baselines must fail on removal of people/building identity, danger cues, exploration structure, or factual era distinctions rather than merely hashing incidental pixels.
8. Browser and installed-desktop product review covers all five scenarios at 100%, 150%, and 200% text scale, representative narrow and wide viewports, keyboard-only navigation, reduced effects, calm mode, no-overflow behavior, focus operation, and pointer map interaction.
9. Primary focus, save/recovery, accessibility, and inspection controls remain immediately findable, visibly keyboard-focused, contrast-compliant, and operable without color, animation, fine pointer control, or sound. Reduced effects removes nonessential motion without erasing factual state.
10. Independent product review must compare the exact candidate against the M7 baseline, reject a superficial coat of paint, and conclude that the map, people, structures, exploration, focus experience, and full UI are substantially more polished, attractive, readable, explorable, and game-like with no factual or accessibility regression.
