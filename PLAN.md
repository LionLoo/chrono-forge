# Chrono Forge v1.0 execution plan

This is the ordered, release-blocking backlog. A milestone is complete only when its observable acceptance criteria pass, its required validation is recorded in `PROGRESS.md`, an independent reviewer has no unresolved release-relevant findings, its PR is green, and it is merged to `main`. Branch names are fixed to keep automation and recovery predictable.

## M0 — Repository and architecture bootstrap

Branch: `bootstrap/repository-harness`

Deliver the pinned React/Vite/Pixi/Tauri toolchain, deterministic module boundaries, local-first persistence decision, project instructions and agents, required documentation, CI/release workflow skeletons, a production-buildable desktop shell, and smoke tests.

Acceptance: clean install; formatting, lint, type checking, unit tests, Rust tests, web E2E smoke, web build, and Windows desktop build pass; app launches; private origin exists; bootstrap PR is independently reviewed, green, and merged.

## M1 — Deterministic world and simulation kernel

Branch: `feature/deterministic-world-kernel`

Implement xoshiro128** streams and golden vectors, integer fixed ticks, canonical state hashing, seeded chunked terrain/water/climate/biomes/resources/habitability/hazards, plants and animals at population-field level, spatial indexes, local A*, event log, worker protocol, and renderer projections.

Acceptance: identical seed/config produces identical initial world and checkpoint hashes in Node, browser main thread, and worker; generated worlds contain reachable habitable regions and meaningful resource variation; no authoritative DOM/wall-clock/random imports; camera pan/zoom and terrain/resource overlays work in the launched app.

## M2 — Focus loop and durable saves

Branch: `feature/focus-and-persistence`

Implement custom durations, presets, start/pause/resume/complete/cancel, optional breaks, calm mode, controlled-clock timer recovery across focus/sleep, configurable focus-to-sim ratio, history/statistics, notifications/sound controls, multiple SQLite save slots, autosave/manual save, seed visibility, import/export, immutable generations, corruption fallback, payload/database migrations, and bounded offline catch-up.

Acceptance: timer controlled-clock tests pass; interruptions never destroy civilization state; save/load and every migration fixture round-trip canonically; corrupt head recovers from an older verified generation; one desktop E2E starts, pauses, resumes, saves, exits, reloads, and completes a session; recap contains only logged events.

## M3 — Autonomous survival, agents, and construction

Branch: `feature/autonomous-settlement`

Implement individual needs, traits, skills, age/health, relationships, summarized memories, personal/communal goals, knowledge, utility/GOAP hybrid decisions, emergent occupations, gathering/storage/transport, food/water/shelter/health/safety, autonomous building demand/site selection/material acquisition/construction/repair/expansion/repurposing/abandonment/demolition, and travel-demand paths.

Acceptance: founding agents survive and autonomously create a viable settlement without player building/job input; choices vary by geography, traits, social state, and deterministic randomness; construction progress and condition are visible; property tests enforce resources, ownership, living-agent actions, placements, finite values, and references across at least 100 cases per PR. Locked M3 evidence uses at least three deterministic seeds through 60,000 ticks, requires every founder alive with sustainable food/water activity and an operational shelter, and proves equivalent results across tick partitions. A 15,000-36,000-tick representative focus window must show a few factual developments and visible incremental construction without completing later societal progression. Packed Worker projections, accessible text, canonical v2 snapshot migration/round trips, real-Worker browser coverage, and save/reload continuation must expose the same authoritative agents, structures, condition, progress, stores, and events. No build or job controls are added.

## M4.5 — Deep interpersonal relationships and family life

Branch: `feature/deep-relationships-family-life`

Implement a versioned, stable-ID, sparse interpersonal graph over the M4 population. Salient bonds retain culturally appropriate typed roles and lifecycle state—including acquaintance, friend, close friend, rival, enemy, mentor/protege, romantic or equivalent partner, long-term partner/spouse or equivalent, separated/broken-up/divorced former partner or equivalent, estranged, reconciled, and widowed states where applicable—plus directional affinity/trust or equivalent state, closeness/commitment/grievance, formation and change ticks, trend, and bounded references to important shared events. Parent-child bonds, siblings, current/former partners, household connections, and practical extended kin are indexed from canonical genealogy and guardianship/family records without materializing every possible kin pair.

Run deterministic relationship opportunities on fixed quotas and total ordering. Formation and evolution use personality and traits, compatibility, proximity, repeated interaction, shared work, factual memories and experiences, culture, kinship, cooperation, conflict, scarcity, migration, political/social events, and controlled per-person/per-bond RNG streams. Positive and negative shared events change future relationship state and behavior; bonds can strengthen, weaken, change category, end, persist as friendship or grievance for decades, estrange, or reconcile. M5 disasters and later systems must consume the same event-backed relationship interface rather than inventing a parallel social model.

Replace static M4 household pairing with deterministic household lifecycles: people can mature into new arrangements, form or join a household through partnership or cultural equivalents, separate, split or merge households, migrate together or apart, care for relatives, receive children, and retain factual membership history. Birth, adoption, guardianship, or other culturally appropriate child-arrival paths require a recorded persistent relationship/family structure and valid household context; arbitrary eligible-adult pairing is forbidden. Lineage and family-resource continuity must remain recognizable across generations.

Locked v1 social-graph budgets: each detailed person has at most 24 active elective social edges and 24 archived relationship summaries. Active partners count toward the active cap but are protected from eviction. Canonical parent/guardian-child records each carry evolving directional family-bond state in a separate O(population) index, so those bonds are more than genealogy and do not consume elective slots; current household membership is likewise indexed. Sibling and extended kin are derived and consume an elective edge only when an emotionally salient bond forms. Each active/archive edge retains at most 16 highest-importance shared source-event IDs, relationship-detail pages contain eight rows, and each person retains the newest 16 household-membership intervals plus one bounded prefix summary. At most eight new-candidate evaluations are scheduled per living detailed person per civil year; explicitly recorded interactions may update only their referenced existing edges. Candidate queues come from spatial, household, work, family, and recent-interaction indexes—never a population-wide pair loop.

When an active elective edge needs a slot, protected active partners are ineligible for eviction and the lowest retention tuple is archived: `(hasMajorEvent, max(closeness, grievance), lastInteractionTick, inverseOtherPersonId)`, compared ascending with exact integer state. When an archive exceeds 24, the same lowest tuple is folded into bounded lifetime counts by final relationship category with first/last ticks; its person-specific detail may disappear only when it is neither protected nor among that person's 24 most significant ended bonds. Shared-event retention keeps the highest `(importance, tick, inverseEventId)` tuples. These total orders, caps, page sizes, and summaries are persisted and tested; changing them is a versioned product decision.

Locked v1 family policies are `pair-bond` and `communal-guardianship`. Partner formation requires living people aged at least 18 in the same settlement, culture-permitted compatibility, reciprocal utility selection, and no parent/child or other ancestor/descendant link, sibling/half-sibling link, aunt-or-uncle/nibling link, or first-cousin link; the default culture permits one active committed partner and a declared plural-partnership culture may permit at most two. A `pair-bond` child arrival requires reciprocal active partner or long-term-partner state, integer trust at least 50 and commitment at least 60 in both directions, a shared or newly formed valid household with capacity/resources, both adults selecting compatible family goals, and the persisted household child-arrival cooldown. A `communal-guardianship` arrival requires that declared cultural policy, one or two living adults aged at least 18 in the same valid household, capacity/resources/cooldown, a selected family/care goal, and—when there are two guardians—an existing partner, close-friend, or direct-kin bond between them. The event records the policy and one or two canonical social parent/guardian IDs; selection is utility-ranked with stable-ID tie-breakers.

Guardianship of an existing dependent after parental death or departure may select at most two living adults from the child's current household, direct kin, or a salient caregiver/friend of the child or parent, subject to the same culture, capacity, care-goal, and deterministic ranking rules. It never creates a new child or retroactive parent. Pairing, child arrival, adoption, and guardianship emit their exact eligibility/policy evidence in typed events so tests and inspection can distinguish culturally valid structure from arbitrary adult selection.

Make represented bonds mechanically consequential. Person-level utility, goals, and plans must use relevant relationships when selecting cooperation/help, resource sharing and caregiving, household and partner formation, child/family decisions, migration companions, knowledge transfer, political support and factions, retaliation/conflict, reconciliation, crisis protection/assistance, and inheritance or family transfer. Relationship influence changes preferences and available plans but does not hard-script outcomes.

Extend bounded Worker projections and React inspection so a deliberate person view exposes parents, children, siblings, partner/equivalent, relevant former partners, household members, close friends, rivals/enemies, and other salient bonds. A keyboard-accessible relationship detail view exposes type/roles, current underlying state, strength/closeness, improving/deteriorating/stable trend, beginning, and pageable significant shared events. Significant formations, transitions, conflicts, reconciliations, separations, births, household changes, and bereavements create immutable typed source events usable by both participants' biographies and factual history; normal focus mode remains uncluttered.

Acceptance: before implementation, check in a locked deterministic scenario catalogue spanning at least three seeds, three generations, and 60 accelerated civil years, plus controlled paired-world comparisons. Across the catalogue—not necessarily in every culture or seed—evidence must demonstrate acquaintance-to-friend-to-close-friend progression; friendship weakening or ending; a durable rivalry or enmity; mentorship; culturally valid partnership and long-term union; separation; estrangement and later reconciliation; widowhood after a recorded death; evolving parent-child and sibling bonds; practical extended-kin lookup; a friendship or grievance persisting at least 20 civil years; dynamic household formation, split/merge, migration, and child arrival; and a recognizable three-generation lineage. Exact scenario seeds, checkpoints, canonical hashes, and operation counts are locked in tests before behavior is accepted.

No birth may select arbitrary adults: every child-arrival record resolves to eligible persistent parent/guardian/partner/family links and a logged culturally valid rule. Controlled identical-state comparisons that differ only in relevant relationship state must prove influence in each of these groups: (1) cooperation/help, resource sharing, caregiving, and crisis assistance; (2) household/partner/child formation and inheritance; (3) migration companions; (4) knowledge transfer; (5) political support or faction alignment; and (6) retaliation/conflict and reconciliation. Outcomes remain utility-based and can differ when stronger needs or constraints dominate, but the relationship contribution and at least one changed selected outcome per group must be observable and deterministic.

Inspection acceptance requires navigating from a person to all required immediate family categories and salient social bonds, then to a relationship history whose displayed values and shared events match authority after save/reload. A biography must truthfully reconstruct at least one multi-decade interpersonal life containing childhood kin or friendship, later partnership/household or equivalent, conflict or estrangement, and reconciliation or bereavement solely from immutable source events.

Canonical snapshot v4 round trips, v3 migration without invented relationships/history, corrupt-head fallback, arbitrary tick-partition/save/Worker equivalence, and browser/native reload flows must preserve graph, genealogy, household history, edge RNG/state, and source references exactly. Invariants reject self-bonds, duplicate canonical bond roles, impossible ancestry cycles, invalid household/partner/guardian references, unlogged child-arrival rules, actions by dead people, dangling event references, and non-finite values. Relationship storage and pulse work are bounded by declared per-person salient-edge/event limits and indexed genealogy/households, with no all-population pair scan; benchmarks record linear-in-people-plus-represented-edges operation counts and an M8 aggregation contract for salient bonds, kinship, households, and histories.

The exact v3-to-v4 mapping is conservative. Existing nonzero `parentIds` become genealogy records with `provenance = migrated-parentage`, unknown historical start, neutral current family-bond state explicitly marked `migrated-unknown`, and no inferred emotional event. Each current household assignment becomes one open membership interval with `provenance = migrated-current-household`, unknown start, and no invented transition. M3 affinity/trust pairs that differ from their initialized 50/50 values, plus the existing M4 cooperation/disagreement/conflict pair, may become `legacy-contact` edges by copying only stored integers and using the migration tick as `observedSinceTick`. Candidate legacy edges sort descending by `(hasSocietyBond, max(abs(affinity-50), abs(trust-50), conflict, cooperation), inverseFirstPersonId, inverseSecondPersonId)` and are greedily admitted only while both endpoints remain below 24. Default complete-graph 50/50 contacts are discarded. Shared household, co-parentage, or a legacy contact never infers friend, rival, partner, spouse, former partner, estrangement, or past relationship events. Legacy contacts cannot satisfy M4.5 lifecycle acceptance until post-migration recorded interaction produces a typed transition.

M8 generalizes this camera-independent detail/aggregate boundary. M5 knowledge remains world-observed during M6; trade and conflict supply contextual evidence without creating a second technology graph.

M6 resolves once per civil year after the M5 development/crisis pulse. Fixed total ordering is founding and residency, community production/consumption, markets and routes, diplomacy, conflict/refugees, conquest/federation/fragmentation, ruins/territory history, then invariants and factual event attachment. No player command chooses diplomacy, war, migration, federation, or conquest. Per-year work is capped at one founding, 64 founding-site samples, 28 diplomacy-pair evaluations, 16 route evaluations, eight migration/refugee choices, four active conflicts/resolutions, 32 factual drafts, eight active settlements, eight active civilizations, 24 retained historical settlements/civilizations, 16 active routes, 32 retained route/conflict summaries, 28 active diplomatic pairs, and 24 ruins. Territory is one fixed tile-owner array plus at most 64 retained ownership changes per settlement; all overflow uses documented deterministic summaries rather than silent factual-event loss.

Locked scenario catalogue uses accelerated 5,000-tick civil years and checkpoints at years 5, 25, 60, and 120. `river-federation` seed `[7,11,13,17]` must autonomously found communities, sustain trade and alliance/federation, and remain peaceful. `ore-coexistence` seed `[19,23,29,31]` must produce culturally distinct independent civilizations, market trade, a factual route disruption and recovery, coexistence, and no war. `storm-conquest` seed `[37,41,43,47]` must produce rivalry, war, refugees, conquest, and conserved territory/history. `renewed-fracture` seed `[53,59,61,67]` must produce federation followed by fragmentation or collapse, an abandoned settlement/ruin, and valid successor history. “Peaceful” means no `conflict-started` event through year 120 plus at least one sustained active trade/alliance relation. “Conflict” means a recorded war begins and resolves through normal utility outcomes. Hashes and exact operation totals are locked only after every semantic assertion passes.

Every checkpoint validates stable names and cultural identity, globally valid memberships/references, conserved people/goods/territory, source-backed chronology, canonical ordering, and bounded operations. Year-120 evidence records settlement/society, development, and M6 operation totals. Arbitrary tick partitions, save/reload, Worker execution, and v5-to-v6 migration must reproduce the same state and hash. Migration creates exactly one provenance-labelled founding settlement/civilization at the saved tick, preserves all v5 state/events byte-for-byte where represented, and invents no M6 event, route, relation, conflict, migration, or ruin. Controlled identical-state comparisons change only one salient relationship and prove a different autonomous co-migration/refugee-support choice and a different faction/political-support/conflict choice.

## M9 — Planetary civilization and space age

Branch: `feature/planetary-space-age`

Implement advanced energy and planetary engineering, planet-scale travel abstraction, contextual spaceflight, spacecraft/orbits/missions, a generated star system and celestial bodies, communication/travel delays, autonomous colonies, cultural divergence, interworld exchange, alien life/civilizations, and diplomacy/cooperation/competition/conflict in space.

Acceptance: accelerated deterministic worlds can autonomously progress from founding through advanced cities to orbit and another world; missions, ships, orbital activity, colonies, transfers, delays, alien contact, and consequences are visible and present in history; at least two seeds demonstrate materially different space development and encounter outcomes.

Locked M9 authority: one world-owned `SpaceState` runs once per civil year after M6 civilization resolution. It owns an immutable generated star system; stable space-local IDs; civilization programs; spacecraft; active and retained missions; due-tick travel, cargo, and communication records; colonies; generated alien societies; space relations; independent persisted system/program/mission/contact RNG streams; and explicit operation counts. Terrestrial `CivilizationsState`, M8 population authority, contextual knowledge, and the factual world event archive remain the sole owners of their existing facts. The space layer receives a bounded read-only context and returns ordered population/resource patches plus factual drafts; `World` applies each patch exactly once. No camera, render state, elapsed-time budget, or player command may select a launch, destination, colony, contact, diplomatic stance, or conflict outcome. The existing alien-signal intervention may alter a documented contact condition but never name or dictate the result.

Advanced-energy and planetary-engineering knowledge must produce inspectable civilization programs and planet-scale transport/launch capacity before physical spaceflight. A launch requires contextual `spaceflight` knowledge plus evidence from an active advanced settlement, institutions, stability, stored material, energy/engineering capacity, and inter-settlement exchange; a bare knowledge flag is insufficient. Ships, missions, arrivals, orbit changes, colony founding, cargo departure/arrival, communication delivery, contact, cooperation, competition, conflict, and resolution use stable integer ticks and total ordering by `(dueTick, id)`. Travel and communication delays derive from immutable body distance and capability. ## M10.9 — World Lifecycle & Save Slot Semantics

Branch: `release/v1-world-lifecycle-save-slots`

Provide three explicit, user-facing operations with distinct semantics: **New World** creates a new slot whose world is initialized from a deterministic fresh seed/configuration; **Duplicate Current World** preserves the active world snapshot in a different slot when cloning is wanted; and **Reset World** replaces the active slot’s state with a fresh initialized world while keeping that slot’s identity. The simulation Worker already initializes through `createInitialWorld(seed, config)`; expose that behavior through `useWorldSimulation` or its typed protocol rather than creating a “new” world by calling `requestSave()` on the current simulation or duplicating world-construction logic in React. Persist `activeSlotId` and settings only after the intended slot operation completes durably. New deterministic seeds must be generated/selected through the product’s established seed policy, displayed to the user, and saved with the resulting world.

Acceptance: creating a New World never inherits the active world’s progressed tick, event history, people, structures, stockpiles, or other simulation state. Existing saves load as before; other slots remain unaffected; active-slot/settings selection is correct across reload; and focus persistence/history remains valid. Focused tests cover fresh-world creation, duplication, reset, existing-save loading, seed determinism, durable active-slot handling, and failure/recovery behavior without deleting or corrupting any existing save.
